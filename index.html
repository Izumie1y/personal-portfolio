02 / Character buildStrengths & weaknesses

Character Build

Traits · habits · reflections

This section reflects how I currently understand myself, including my strengths, learning preferences, and the areas I know I still need to work on.

Personal strengths map

Strengths I Recognize

  • Humor: I often use humor to handle situations and connect with people.
  • Curiosity: I enjoy gathering information and understanding how things work.
  • Willingness to learn: I ask questions and seek guidance when I don't understand something.
  • Adaptability: I can adjust and move forward after disappointment instead of dwelling on it indefinitely.
  • Research and following instructions: I am comfortable finding information and completing clearly defined tasks.

Areas I Want to Improve

  • Discipline and consistency: I tend to rely on short bursts of work and deadlines.
  • Initiative: I want to contribute ideas more readily instead of waiting for others to lead.
  • Confidence: I sometimes hold back when I feel less knowledgeable than the people around me.
  • Focus: I can become distracted, especially when a task does not interest me.
  • Artistic skills: I want to develop the technical ability to bring my ideas to life.

How I Understand Myself

How I Approach Life

Humor, curiosity, and kindness are qualities I value in myself. I often use humor to handle situations and connect with others. I'm naturally curious and enjoy learning new things, while I'm still figuring out how to practice kindness consistently in everyday life.

How I Learn

I find it easier to understand new information when I can see it. Diagrams, demonstrations, visual examples, and organized notes help me process ideas. I'm also more motivated when I can connect what I'm learning to something that genuinely interests me.

What I'm Working On

I've come to recognize that consistency, focus, and confidence are areas I need to improve. I sometimes struggle to stay motivated when a task doesn't interest me, and I can hesitate to share my ideas when I'm unsure of myself. These are things I'm gradually learning to work on.

Personal Reflections

One thing I've realized is that I often focus more on the result than the process. I feel motivated when I'm interested in a task or can see a good outcome, but I sometimes settle for completing the requirements instead of exploring how much I can improve the work.

I've also learned that developing artistic skills takes patience. Coming from a STEM background, I was used to understanding some topics relatively quickly. Art has shown me that knowing what I want to create is different from having the skills to execute it. I'm learning to accept being a beginner and to treat practice as part of the process.

During group work, I found that I could contribute by researching and completing assigned tasks, but I sometimes hesitated to share ideas or take initiative. I want to become more confident in expressing my thoughts and more comfortable contributing even when I'm unsure.

I don't need to have everything figured out yet. I need to keep learning, practicing, and becoming more willing to try.
